3. What Personal Data We Process

Depending on how you use our Services, we may collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Fan Photo Submissions: When using our Fan Photos or Greenbox services, we collect your digital image (selfie) and any metadata associated with the photo upload, including the date and event identifier.
  • Contact Information: If you register for newsletters, contact us, or participate in raffles/games, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and social media handles.
  • Interactive Game & Raffle Records: Data regarding your answers to trivia, participation times, scores, and raffle tickets earned or won.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, device model, operating system type, browser type, interaction logs, conversion metrics, and cookie data collected during your session.

4. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases and for these specified purposes:

  • Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)): We process your photo uploads, face matching profiles, and marketing emails exclusively based on your explicit consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Performance of a Contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): Processing contact and billing information to provide purchased SaaS solutions, custom API packages, or deliver physical/digital raffle prizes.
  • Legitimate Interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): Processing analytical and technical usage data to secure our applications, improve network scalability, measure business ROI, and optimize our global mapping interface.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processors

To provide high-fidelity fan experiences and capture business value, we may share your data with:

  • Event Organizers & Brand Sponsors: Anonymized or consented visual and engagement data is shared with the hosting sports clubs, arenas, or brands to measure campaign ROI and broadcast images onto live stadium screens.
  • Infrastructure & Hosting Providers: We use secure, EU-based cloud providers to store image files and technical database records securely.
  • Analytical Partners: Third-party tools mapping fan network conversions, website metrics, and usage tracking in compliance with GDPR.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to unverified third parties under any circumstances.

6. Data Retention Periods

We only keep your personal data for as long as is necessary to fulfill the processing purposes:

  • Fan Photos & Selfies: Automatically archived or securely deleted within 30 days after the conclusion of the specific event campaign, unless the User actively requests longer storage or provides marketing consent.
  • Raffle & Game Data: Retained for 90 days following winner selection to resolve shipping or disputes.
  • Contact & Marketing Data: Retained until the user unsubscribes or requests deletion.

7. Your GDPR Rights

As a data subject, you possess full rights under Chapter III of the GDPR:

  • Right of Access: Request a comprehensive copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request immediate deletion of your data when consent is withdrawn.
  • Right to Restriction: Limit the processing of your data under specific statutory conditions.
  • Right to Portability: Obtain a structured, machine-readable export of the data you provided to us.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our data protection team via info@seyusolutions.com. We will respond within one month of receiving your request.

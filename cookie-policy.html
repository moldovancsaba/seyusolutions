Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. What Are Cookies, and What We Currently Use

Cookies are small text files stored directly on your computer, mobile device, or tablet when you visit a website. They allow a site to recognize your browser and remember settings between visits.

As of the date above, the Seyu Solutions website does not set any cookies. The only browser storage in use is a single local storage entry on the founder profile page that remembers a visitor's light/dark display preference. This value stays on your device — it is never transmitted to us, to any partner, or to any third party.

2. Categories We May Use in the Future

If we introduce cookies or similar tracking technologies later, they will fall into one or more of the following categories. We will update this policy — and, where required by law, request your consent — before any such technology goes live:

  • Essential / Strictly Necessary Cookies: Required for core site functionality, such as security or session handling.
  • Performance and Analytical Cookies: Anonymous information about how visitors interact with the site, used to improve page performance and content.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember visitor preferences, such as language or display settings.
  • Targeting / Advertising Cookies: Placed by third-party networks, with explicit consent, to measure campaign performance.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not currently use Google Analytics, social media pixels, or any other third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising service on this website. If that changes, this section will be updated to name the specific service before it is deployed.

4. How to Manage Your Browser Settings

You can control and clear local storage and cookies at any time through your browser settings — most browsers let you view, block, or delete stored site data. Clearing the founder page's stored theme preference simply resets it to the default; no other part of the site depends on it.

For more detailed information on configuring these preferences in your browser, please visit:

  • Apple Safari Preferences

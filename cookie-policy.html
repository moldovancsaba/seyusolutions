Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored directly on your computer, mobile device, or tablet when you visit our website or interact with our digital platforms. They allow the website to recognize your browser, save specific user settings, and provide advanced interactive layouts without breaking performance metrics.

In addition to standard cookies, we may use other tracking technologies, such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage mechanisms, to measure brand engagement and overall ROI.

2. How We Use Cookies

Seyu Solutions uses cookies to ensure our application runs with absolute vertical rhythm and rigid reliability. We categorize the cookies we use into four functional groups:

  • Essential / Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are critical to navigating our site and utilizing core features, such as accessing secure portals or submitting fan selfies. The platform cannot function correctly without these, and they do not process any directly identifiable marketing data.
  • Performance and Analytical Cookies: These gather anonymous information about how our visitors interact with the site, including which pages are visited most often and any errors encountered. This allows us to calibrate conversion funnels, evaluate page speed indices, and optimize our interactive world map.
  • Functional Cookies: These enable the website to remember decisions you make (such as language preference or location coordinates) to offer a more customized, fluid experience.
  • Targeting / Advertising Cookies: Placed by verified third-party networks (with your explicit consent) to map fan community engagements, evaluate sponsor banner click-rates, and deliver targeted advertising campaigns aligned with your interests.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Our partners and verified third-party services may set cookies through our website to help us manage campaigns and measure fan demographics:

  • Google Analytics: Provides anonymous technical metrics on user flow, traffic sources, and session durations to help us improve user interface experiences.
  • Social Media Pixels (e.g., Meta, LinkedIn): Allow sports clubs and brand sponsors to trace conversions of social sharing actions triggered by fan photo downloads.

4. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You have the absolute right to control and refuse the use of cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can change your browser settings to reject new cookies, disable existing ones, or notify you when new ones are sent.

Please note that if you choose to completely block all cookies (including essential ones), certain parts of Seyu Solutions, such as interactive Greenbox photo captures, active raffles, and dynamic UI animations, may become partially or fully unavailable.

For more detailed information on configuring cookie preferences in your browser, please visit:

  • Google Chrome Preferences
  • Apple Safari Preferences
  • Mozilla Firefox Preferences

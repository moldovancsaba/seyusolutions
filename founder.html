01 The founder

From the stands to the pitch-side LED

Before Seyu, Tom spent more than a decade in international online and offline marketing and leadership roles across the sport and FMCG sectors — experience he channelled into building a platform fans, clubs and sponsors could all believe in. He co-founded Seyu in Budapest in 2018, then grew it from an MVP tested at four Hungarian events into a company powering fan moments in 42 countries.

I couldn't imagine my life without sports or technological solutions.

Tom "Vechy" Vecsernyes
CEO & Co-Founder, Seyu
02 The story so far

From MVP to a global fan-engagement platform

Four milestones on the way from a Budapest startup to Seyu today.

Tom's timeline at Seyu
1
2018 · Founded

Tom co-founds Seyu in Budapest, bringing a decade of sport and FMCG marketing experience into sports tech.

2
2019 · Scaling up

The team grows to ten, live at major handball, water polo and football events — including Telekom Veszprém, DVSC, Ferencváros TC and MOL Fehérvár FC — alongside a collaboration with Microsoft's Global Sport Innovation Center.

3
2019 · Recognised

Hiventures names Seyu one of Hungary's most promising startups of 2019, backing the company's early growth.

4
Today · Global reach

Under Tom's leadership Seyu now connects 1M+ fans across 4000+ events, 42 countries and 28 sports — with offices in New York, London, Budapest and Szeged.

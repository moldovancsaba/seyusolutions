About

Founder, marketer, lifelong fan.

For more than a decade, Tom worked in international online and offline marketing and leadership roles across the sport and FMCG sectors — experience that shaped how he thinks about audiences, brands, and what actually makes a fan feel close to the action. In 2018 he co-founded Seyu in Budapest, turning that experience into a real-time moderated, branded, fan-generated content platform.

Under his leadership, Seyu grew from an MVP tested at four Hungarian events into a platform live at 4000+ events across 42 countries and 28 sports — trusted by federations, clubs and sponsors including RFEF, EuroLeague, CHL and Wasps Rugby.

How Seyu Works

From a fan's phone to the big screen.

Five steps take a fan from the stands — or the sofa — to the jumbotron, in real time.

  1. 1

    Snap

    Fans take a photo at the venue or from home — no app needed.

  2. 2

    Message

    They add a motivating message for their stars and their team.

  3. 3

    Moderate

    A real-time moderated channel checks every post — brand-safe by default.

  4. 4

    Display

    It appears in branded frames on the jumbotron, LEDs, broadcast or stream.

  5. 5

    Cheer

    Fans see themselves at the heart of the action. Together for victory.

The Company

Seyu Solutions Kft.

A Budapest-based fan-engagement company. Seyu partners with clubs, federations and sponsors to turn fans into real-time, branded, moderated content — live on the jumbotron, pitch-side LEDs, broadcast and social, at every venue.

Whether the goal is brand awareness, lead generation or a stronger connection between fans and their stars, the approach is the same: real fans, real time, always on-brand.

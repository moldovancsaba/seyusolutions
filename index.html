01 What we do

Every fan's moment, on the big screen

We believe every sport-loving fan should get to show their devotion to their stars and connect with fellow fans around the world — however far they are from the action. So we built the platform that makes it happen, in real time and always on-brand.

Real-time moderation

Every photo passes through our real-time moderated channel before it reaches any screen — always brand-safe.

Your branded frames

Fans appear in your frames — your colours, crest and sponsor lock-up on every post.

Every screen

Jumbotron, pitch-side LEDs, broadcast and live streaming — one moderated post, every surface.

From anywhere

In the stands or on the sofa — fans join from wherever they are, with no app to download.
